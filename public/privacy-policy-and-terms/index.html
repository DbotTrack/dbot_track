1. Who we are and what this Policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Dbotics collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you use the Services. It applies to our websites, our web app, and the Dbot Track Android App.

Dbot Track is a construction-execution platform used by construction companies to monitor project progress, primarily through on-site staff (QS, QC, Site Engineers, HSE, Project Managers, and similar roles) and management teams.

Our Services may link to third-party websites or services. Those third parties are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

This Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions and any separate agreement (Order Form) between Dbotics and your organization.

2. Our role: your organization's data vs. our own data

Dbot Track is provided primarily to organizations, and this affects who is responsible for your data.

  • Customer Data (organization as Data Fiduciary). When you use Dbot Track through an organization (your employer or a Dbotics customer), that organization decides what data is uploaded and how it is used. For that data, the organization is the Data Fiduciary and Dbotics acts as a Data Processor, processing Customer Data only to provide and support the Services under our agreement with the organization and on its instructions. Requests about Customer Data are usually directed to your organization.
  • Dbotics-controlled data (Dbotics as Data Fiduciary). We act as an independent Data Fiduciary for certain data we determine the purpose and means of — for example website enquiries, account administration and authentication records, security and audit logs, product analytics, and support communications.

3. Information we collect

We collect information in three ways: information you or your organization provide, information created in the Services, and information collected automatically.

A. Information you or your organization provide

  • Account and profile details — name, email address, phone number, role/designation, organization name, and any additional profile fields your organization enables.
  • Invitation, administration, and access-permission details.
  • Support and other communications you send to us.

B. Information created in the Services ("Customer Data")

Depending on how your organization configures Dbot Track, this may include:

  • Project and site details;
  • Progress and execution records (updates, quantities, checklists, QC/inspection records, observations, issues/NCRs, comments, approvals);
  • Safety (HSE) records, as configured;
  • Photos, videos, drawings, documents, attachments, and notes/annotations;
  • Activity history (who did what, and when).

C. Location information (precise; foreground only)

Where your organization enables it and your device permissions allow it, the App collects precise (GPS) location only while the App is open and in active use (in the foreground), to support site-based reporting and location-aware workflows. We do not collect location in the background or when the App is closed. You can turn off location permission at any time in your device settings; some location-aware features may then not work.

D. Information collected automatically

  • Device and network information — IP address, device type, operating system, browser/App version, language, and device identifiers.
  • Usage and diagnostic information — screens/pages viewed, features used, timestamps, and performance/crash/error logs.
  • Cookies and similar technologies on our websites and web app, and equivalent SDK identifiers in the App (see Analytics and cookies).

4. How we use information, and our legal bases

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate the Services — accounts, workflows, collaboration, and reporting;
  • Authenticate users and secure the Services — sign-in, monitoring, and investigating suspicious activity or misuse;
  • Provide customer support — responding to requests and troubleshooting;
  • Improve and develop the Services — product analytics and performance improvements;
  • Communicate with you — transactional messages such as OTPs, invitations, and password resets. We do not currently send marketing communications; if we introduce them, we will obtain consent where required and provide a way to opt out;
  • Comply with law and protect rights — meeting legal obligations, enforcing our Terms, and protecting the rights, safety, and security of Dbotics, our customers, users, and others.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), we process personal data on the basis of your consent and/or on the basis of legitimate uses permitted by law (for example, where you have voluntarily provided data for a specified purpose, and for the performance of our services, security, and legal compliance). Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time (see Your rights); withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

5. Analytics and cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies on our websites and web app for essential functions such as maintaining sessions and remembering preferences.

We may use third-party product analytics tools to understand how the Services are used and to improve them. Such providers may process usage events and device/SDK identifiers on our behalf. We may change or add analytics providers over time and will update this Policy accordingly. We do not use analytics for third-party advertising.

6. How we share information

We do not sell personal data. We share information only as needed to run the Services, comply with law, and protect rights and safety.

Service providers / subprocessors. We share information with vendors who process it on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and security obligations:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — hosting and storage (files may be stored in Amazon S3);
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (processes recipient email address and email content to send OTPs, invitations, and password resets);
  • Product analytics providers (see Analytics and cookies);
  • Google Workspace — our internal business email and communications.

Legal and safety. We may disclose information where we reasonably believe it is necessary to comply with applicable law or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Dbotics, our customers, users, and others.

Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, insolvency, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections and this Policy.

Dbot Track is hosted on AWS US East (N. Virginia). This means personal data may be stored and processed in the United States and accessed from other locations where we or our service providers operate. Where we transfer personal data outside India, we do so in accordance with the DPDP Act and applicable law, and we require our service providers to protect it under contractual safeguards.

8. Data retention

  • Customer Data. We generally retain Customer Data for the duration of the customer's subscription or contract. When the contract ends, or the customer requests deletion, we will delete or de-identify Customer Data within 90 days, unless a longer period is required for legal, security, or operational reasons.
  • Account and Dbotics-controlled data. We retain this only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy or as required by law.
  • Backups and logs. Information may persist in backups or security logs for a limited period before it is overwritten or deleted in the ordinary course.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS);
  • Role-based access controls;
  • Audit logs and activity history for key actions.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please keep your credentials confidential and use a strong, unique password. If we become aware of a personal-data breach affecting you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by the DPDP Act.

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access — obtain confirmation of, and a summary of, the personal data we process about you and how we process it;
  • Correction and updating — have inaccurate or incomplete personal data corrected or completed;
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data where it is no longer required (see Account and data deletion);
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on consent, withdraw it at any time;
  • Nominate — nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, as provided under the DPDP Act;
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint about our handling of your personal data (see Grievance redressal).

How to exercise your rights.

  • Organization-managed accounts: because your organization controls most Customer Data, many requests are handled through your organization's administrator. We will assist our customer organizations in responding.
  • Direct requests to Dbotics: email privacy@dbottrack.com. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law. We may need to verify your identity, and we may decline or limit a request where we are permitted or required to retain information for legal, security, or operational reasons — we will explain our reasons if we do.

11. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your Dbot Track account and associated personal data through any of the following, without needing to reinstall the App:

  • In the App / web app: go to Settings → Account → Request account deletion; or
  • By email: contact privacy@dbottrack.com with the subject line "Account deletion request".

What happens next. Because Dbot Track accounts are typically created and managed by an organization, we may need to verify your request and, where the account is organization-managed, coordinate with or seek confirmation from your organization's administrator. Once verified, we will delete or de-identify your personal data associated with the account within 90 days, except where we are required to retain certain information for legal, security, or operational reasons (for example, records we must keep to comply with law, resolve disputes, or maintain security). Certain Customer Data owned by your organization may be retained by, exported to, or deleted at the direction of your organization in accordance with our agreement with it. We will tell you what, if anything, we must retain and why.
